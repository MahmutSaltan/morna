Frequently asked questions

How do I cancel my subscription?
Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Morna and tap Cancel Subscription. You can also tap "Manage subscription" in Morna's settings.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?
Sign in with the same Apple ID, open Morna and tap "Restore purchases" in settings or on the subscription screen.

How do I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Why did my streak reset after reinstalling?
Your progress is stored only on your device, so deleting the app removes it.

Reminders don't arrive.
Check that notifications for Morna are allowed in Settings › Notifications › Morna.
