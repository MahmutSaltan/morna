Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Morna ("the app") is made by Mahmut Saltan. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version

Morna has no accounts and no servers of its own. What you enter, such as your name, routines, streak and progress, stays on your device. We send anonymous usage events to improve the app, and Apple and RevenueCat handle subscriptions. We never sell your data and never use it for advertising.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your optional name, preferences, custom routines, session history, streak and rewards on your device, and shares a small summary with the Morna widget on the same device. This data is never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No notification data leaves your device.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. To check whether you have an active subscription, the app uses RevenueCat, Inc., which receives a random app user ID, your purchase history and basic device information such as country and app version. This information is not linked to your name or contact details. RevenueCat privacy policy.

Usage analytics

To understand how the app is used and improve it, Morna sends anonymous usage events to Mixpanel, Inc. (for example: onboarding step completed, routine started or finished, paywall viewed, trial started). Events carry a random app user ID, device model, OS and app version, and country. We do not send your name or the names of your routines, and Mixpanel does not store your IP address. Data is stored on Mixpanel's servers in the European Union. Mixpanel privacy policy.

What we do not do

No advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, no location, no contacts, no selling or sharing of personal data.

Legal basis and international transfers

Subscription data is processed to provide the service you purchased. Anonymous usage analytics are processed on the basis of our legitimate interest in improving the app. RevenueCat may process data in the United States.

Retention

Data on your device stays until you delete the app. Analytics events are kept by Mixpanel for as long as needed to analyse app usage and are then deleted.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and Turkish data protection law (KVKK) you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to its processing. Data on your device is fully under your control. For data held by RevenueCat or Mixpanel, email us at mahmutsaltan01@gmail.com and we will handle the request.

Children

Morna is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Health

Morna is for general wellness and is not medical advice. If you have a health condition, check with your doctor before exercising.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
